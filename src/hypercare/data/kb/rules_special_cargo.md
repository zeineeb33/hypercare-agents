# Special cargo rules: HAZ and SOC

## Shipper-owned containers (SOC)
A price line flagged **SOC** applies when the customer ships in its own container instead of Northwind equipment.
SOC rates are lower than standard rates because Northwind does not provide, reposition or maintain the container.
On Le Havre to Shanghai, for example, the SOC line is usually 150 to 250 USD below the standard 40HC rate.
SOC lines only accept general cargo.

## Hazardous cargo (HAZ)
Hazardous goods (IMO classes 1 to 9) can only be quoted on a price line flagged **HAZ**.
A HAZ surcharge is added on top of the base rate, and a dangerous goods declaration is required before booking.

## Combining HAZ and SOC
**Hazardous cargo cannot be quoted on a SOC line.** SOC lines are not approved for dangerous goods.
If the customer wants to ship hazardous cargo in its own container, the request must go to the Pricing desk
for a special rate (see the escalation process). Otherwise, quote the cargo on a HAZ line with Northwind equipment.
