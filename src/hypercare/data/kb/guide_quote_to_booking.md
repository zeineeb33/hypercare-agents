# From quote to booking

## Quote validity
A quote is valid for **14 days** from its creation date. The validity date is printed on the quote.

## Converting a quote
1. Open the quote in QuotePort and check its status is ACCEPTED or PENDING.
2. Click **Convert to booking** and select a voyage.
3. Confirm the equipment and quantity. The booking total is the quoted all-in price per container multiplied by
   the number of containers.

## Expired quotes
An expired quote cannot be converted. Create a new quote: it will use the current ACTIVE price line,
so the price may differ from the expired quote.
