# Hypercare escalation process

Hypercare answers questions that are covered by the documentation or by the data. Everything else is escalated.

| Situation | Escalate to |
|---|---|
| Rate confirmation or booking on a DRAFT line | Pricing Manager |
| Overlapping ACTIVE lines | Pricing desk (pricing-desk@northwind.example) |
| Discounts, volume deals, special rates (including HAZ in SOC) | Pricing Manager |
| Pricing question not answered by the documentation | Pricing desk |
| Application errors, slowness, outages | IT service desk (servicedesk@northwind.example) |

When escalating, include the ticket reference, the price line or quote reference and what was already checked.
