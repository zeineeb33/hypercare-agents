# Hypercare FAQ

**What are the hypercare hours?** Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 19:00 CET.

**Who manages routing data?** The routing support team (routing-support@northwind.example). Missing or wrong routes
in the routing tool are fixed by them, usually within one business day.

**Who manages commodity data?** The commodity data team (commodity-data@northwind.example).

**QuotePort is slow or down. What do I do?** Check the IT status page and open an incident with the IT service desk.
Hypercare does not manage application incidents.

**Where are the release notes?** In the knowledge base, under Release notes (TideRate 3.2 and 3.3).
