# Demo video: Dynamic pricing (transcript)

[00:00] Welcome to this short demo of dynamic pricing in TideRate 3.3. Dynamic pricing is in beta on the North
Europe to Asia trade only.

[00:25] Screen: the price line screen, with a new column "Dynamic" next to the 40HC rate.

[00:40] Dynamic prices are recalculated **every six hours** from vessel utilisation and recent bookings.

[01:10] The system never goes below the **floor price**. The floor is the higher of two values: the cost of the
voyage leg plus the minimum margin of 8 percent, and 90 percent of the published ACTIVE rate.

[01:45] Screen: a chart of the dynamic 40HC rate over two weeks, with a dashed horizontal line labelled "Floor".

[02:05] Sales see the dynamic price in QuotePort with a "Dynamic" badge. The quote keeps that price until its
validity date, even if the dynamic price changes afterwards.

[02:30] To switch a line back to a static rate, the Pricing Manager disables dynamic pricing on the line.
